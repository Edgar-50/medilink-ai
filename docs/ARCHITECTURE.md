# Architecture

## Domains

### Identity
Patients, doctors and administrators.

### Care coordination
Appointments, schedules, encounters, referrals and follow-ups.

### Clinical records
Health-record timeline, observations, medications, laboratory data and documents.

### Communication
Secure messaging and future telemedicine.

### Intelligence
Patient Copilot, Clinical Copilot, document intelligence, predictive analytics and explainability.

## AI rule

AI-generated clinical content must be treated as assistive output. Clinician-facing generated notes and summaries should require human review before they become part of a medical record.

## Data rule

Use synthetic data for the public portfolio repository. Do not commit real patient data, secrets, access tokens or identifiable health information.
